Shipping & Express Delivery

We provide insured Australia-wide express shipping and tracked worldwide dispatch directly from our curated boutique hubs.

  • Australia Express: 2 - 4 business days (Free on orders above $150 AUD)
  • Standard Delivery: 4 - 7 business days ($10 AUD flat rate)
  • International Courier: Express air courier with full end-to-end tracking

All garments are shipped in protective dust covers with verified Silk Mark authenticity tags.

14-Day Returns & Exchanges

Your complete satisfaction is our priority. We offer a 14-day boutique exchange guarantee on all unworn items.

  • Garments must have original designer tags and security seals intact.
  • Items can be exchanged online or returned at any flagship boutique.
  • Direct refund or boutique store credit processed within 24 hours of inspection.
